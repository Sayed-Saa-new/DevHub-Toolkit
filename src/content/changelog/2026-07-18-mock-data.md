Meet **`/t/mock-data`** — a full mock data workbench for seeding databases, prototyping UIs, and writing tests.

## Schema builder

Compose any shape from **30+ field types** — UUID, names, emails, phones, addresses, IPs, dates, integers with min/max, booleans, custom pick-lists, and more.

![Mock Data Generator schema builder](/img/changelog/mock-data-schema.png)

## Deterministic output, five formats

Pick row count (1–5000) and a seed — same seed always produces the same data. Export as **JSON**, **CSV**, **SQL INSERT**, **TypeScript**, or **XML** with one-click copy and download.

![Mock Data Generator JSON output](/img/changelog/mock-data-output.png)

Everything runs client-side. No signup, no upload.
