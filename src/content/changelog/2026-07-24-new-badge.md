title: "'New' badge for freshly shipped tools"
date: 2026-07-24
version: v1.10
tag: New feature
---

Freshly added tools now wear a small **New** badge in the sidebar and the ⌘K command palette, so you can spot what just landed at a glance.

- Shown next to any tool marked `isNew` in the registry
- Disappears automatically the first time you open that tool
- Tracked locally in your browser (`devhub:seen`) — nothing leaves your device
- Works across sidebar navigation and the global command palette

No setup required — just open DevHub and follow the badges to the newest utilities.
