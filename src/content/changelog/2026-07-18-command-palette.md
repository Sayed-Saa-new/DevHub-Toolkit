The **Global Command Palette** is now the fastest way to move around DevHub Toolkit.

## What's new

- **Recents & Favorites** show up automatically when the palette is empty.
- **Category groups** — tools are neatly split by Converters, Generators, Design, Editors, Reference, and AI.
- **Inline star toggle** — favorite any tool without leaving the palette.
- **Router-driven navigation** — SPA transitions, no full reloads.
- **Quick actions** — jump Home, open Favorites, view Shortcuts, or hit GitHub.

> Press <kbd>⌘K</kbd> (or <kbd>Ctrl K</kbd>) from anywhere to try it.

A small quality-of-life change that pays off every session.
