Meet **`/t/json-to-ts`** — a batteries-included JSON → TypeScript converter with a serious feature list:

- Output modes: **interface**, **type**, or **Zod schema**
- Custom **root name**
- Toggle `T[]` vs `Array<T>`
- **Readonly** properties
- **Export** modifier
- **Null → optional** conversion
- **Quoted keys** mode
- **Nested type hoisting** with automatic naming
- Cross-array **union merging**
- One-click **copy** + **download**

No signup, no upload — everything runs locally in your browser.
