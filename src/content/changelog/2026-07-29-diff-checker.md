A new **Diff Checker** joins the Editors shelf. Paste two blobs, drop two files, or upload from disk — see exactly what changed with line-, word-, character- or sentence-level precision.

## What's inside

- **4 granularities** — line, word, character, sentence
- **2 views** — synced side-by-side split, or classic unified
- **Smart options** — ignore case, ignore whitespace, trim lines, hide blank lines, wrap long lines, changes-only mode
- **Live stats bar** — added / removed / unchanged / similarity %
- **Jump between changes** with `J`/`K` (or `N`/`P`) and the up/down buttons
- **File input** — drag-and-drop or picker for `.txt`, `.md`, `.json`, `.js`, `.ts`, `.css`, `.sql`, `.log` and more (up to 5 MB)
- **Editable filenames** feed straight into the exported patch header
- **Unified `.diff` export** — copy to clipboard or download as a real patch file
- **`⌘S` swap sides**, one-click sample, session persistence in `localStorage`

Everything runs 100% client-side — no upload, no signup, no rate limit.
