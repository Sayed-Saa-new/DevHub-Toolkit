A brand-new **Favicon Generator** — build a complete favicon pack in seconds.

- **From text or image** — type 1–3 letters (font, weight, colors) or upload a PNG / SVG / JPG
- **Shape & padding** — square, rounded (adjustable radius), or circle, with transparent-background support
- **Live preview** at 16 / 32 / 64 / 180 / 512 px, plus a browser-tab mock
- **Full export pack** — `favicon.ico` (16/32/48 multi-size), 11 PNG sizes, `apple-touch-icon.png`, `android-chrome-192/512`, `site.webmanifest`, and a ready-to-paste `<head>` snippet — all zipped
- 100% client-side, no upload, no signup
