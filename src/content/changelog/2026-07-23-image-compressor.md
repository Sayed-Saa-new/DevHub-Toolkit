Added **Image Compressor** (`/t/image-compressor`) — compress JPEG, PNG, WebP and AVIF right in your browser.

- Batch upload with drag-and-drop
- Adjustable quality, max dimension and target size (MB)
- Convert to **WebP / JPEG / PNG** on the fly
- Live before/after size with % saved per file and totals
- One-click download (individual or all)
- Runs 100% locally — files never leave your device

Great for shrinking screenshots, hero images, or blog assets before uploading.
