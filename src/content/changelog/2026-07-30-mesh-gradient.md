A brand-new **Mesh Gradient Generator** for hero backgrounds, marketing sites and product screenshots.

- **Drag-to-position** color points directly on the canvas (up to 8)
- **6 curated presets** — Aurora, Sunset, Mint, Peach, Monochrome, Vaporwave
- **Grain overlay** slider for that premium, filmic look
- **Three export formats** — CSS, React/JSX, SVG — plus one-click **PNG** (1600×1000) and **SVG** download
- Fully client-side, no signup, ⌘R to randomize
